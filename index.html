About

Strategy on one side. A camera on the other.

I'm a social media manager and cinematographer based in Pune. I plan the content, write the hooks and captions, shoot the footage, edit the reels, publish, and read the numbers afterwards.

Most teams split that work across four people. I've done it end to end for school admissions, sports organisations and a community listening initiative. Fewer hand-offs means faster turnaround and content that looks like one idea.

Every post I make is part of a campaign. It has an audience, a job to do, and a way to be measured.

My process

Eight stages, one loop.

The last stage feeds the first. What the numbers show shapes the next round of research.

01

Research

Study the brand, its audience and what similar accounts post before any idea is written.

02

Strategy

Set the goal, the audience and the content pillars every post has to serve.

03

Planning

Build the calendar, scripts and shot lists, with formats matched to each platform.

04

Creation

Shoot, design and edit in-house so the story stays consistent from idea to final cut.

05

Publishing

Post at the right time, with hooks, captions and calls to action written for the platform.

06

Community

Reply to comments and messages quickly, in the brand's own voice.

07

Analytics

Track reach, engagement, impressions and growth against the original goal.

08

Optimisation

Keep what worked, drop what didn't, and carry the findings into the next plan.

My approach

How I think about social media.

Strategy

Every post has a job. Before I plan content I want to know the audience, the goal and what we want people to do next.

Creativity

A good idea is specific. I look for the detail that is true to the brand, then build the hook, the frame and the edit around it.

Consistency

Audiences trust brands that show up. A calendar, a recognisable look and a steady posting rhythm matter more than any single post.

Performance

Numbers decide what I do next. I read reach, engagement and growth, keep what works and change what doesn't.

I'd rather show you what a post did than tell you how good it looks.
